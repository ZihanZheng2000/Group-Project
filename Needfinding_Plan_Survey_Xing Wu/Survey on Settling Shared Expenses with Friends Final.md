# Survey on Settling Shared Expenses with Friends

*Needfinding survey  English version*

## Before you begin

Please think of **one or more times in the past year** when you and friends took part in an activity and needed to settle shared costs afterward. For each experience you draw on, **at least 3 people in total** shared **at least two expenses**, and at least one person paid upfront. You can include experiences involving Splitwise, another tool, a group chat, or just conversation. **Different questions may draw on different qualifying experiences. In the questions below, “these experiences” means the qualifying experiences you can remember. Select everything you remember happening; you do not need to guess about experiences you cannot recall.** When a question asks for one example, choose one you remember clearly.

We will not ask for names, account details, or exact amounts. All questions are multiple-choice except the last two, which are optional written answers. For questions marked Select all that apply, you may choose more than one answer. An answer marked Select alone must not be combined with other answers. Choose I cannot remember alone only when you cannot recall enough to select any other answer to that question.
